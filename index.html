DAILY OPERATION

How to use your Solar Suitcase

Once assembled, everyday operation follows the same four steps.

1
Charge it up

Unfold the solar panel in direct sunlight, or plug the wall charger into any 15A socket to top up the battery.

2
Check the readings

Use the voltage sensor and Smart BMS monitor to confirm charge level and pack health before connecting a load.

3
Switch on and connect

Turn the relay module ON and connect your output cable to the device or vehicle you're powering.

4
Shut down safely

Switch the relay OFF and disconnect the cable before folding the panel and packing the suitcase away.
